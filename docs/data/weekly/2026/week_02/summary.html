Subject: 🏈 HFL Week 2 — William wins by how much?

Owners of the Happy Fun League,

Week 2 also gave us a 71-point winner, a 100-point loser, and two teams still unbeaten. Marliese and Alana are 2–0. They took rather different routes to get there.

Two weeks in

  • Still unbeaten: Slater Chatty Cathies and Arizona Smol Puppers. Marliese has won by 10 and 33; Alana by 5 and 3. There are several ways to get to 2–0.
  • Still looking for a win: Big dog Fin and Philly Fly Eagles Fly. Finley’s three-point loss was the closest either has come so far.
  • Everybody else: Eight teams at 1–1. Any confident assessment of the standings can probably wait another week.

The league scored 1,055 points, down from 1,174 in Week 1. Josh Allen’s 39 led the individual scoring. Andrea’s 34 was a team total. I will leave those two facts next to each other and move on.
