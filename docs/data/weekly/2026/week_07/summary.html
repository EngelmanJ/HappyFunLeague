Subject: 🏈 HFL Week 7 — Finley has caught the crowd

Owners of the Happy Fun League,

Three weeks ago, Finley was 0–4. Now he’s 3–4, alongside four other teams, after beating Ellis by 26. Those of us who got to play him in September should probably stop offering advice.

The Wombats and Trout are back to sharing the best record at 5–2. Alana had the biggest win, Phil had his best score of the season, and William won the closest game. Nobody finished within eleven points of anybody else, so it was a fairly peaceful week to check the scores. Depending on which side you were on.
