Subject: 🏈 HFL Week 3 — So much for the undefeated teams

Owners of the Happy Fun League,

Last week we had two unbeaten teams to keep an eye on. Davis and Phil have taken care of that, and now eight teams are tied at 2–1. I hope nobody spent much time arranging a power ranking.

Davis had the week’s highest score, Ellis had the biggest win, and Phil won the only game decided by fewer than ten points. For the rest of us, the standings offer plenty of company and very little clarity.

Highlights at a glance

  • Highest team score: Big Dog D, 118.
  • Player of the Week: Jahmyr Gibbs, 33 points.
  • Closest game: Pray for Mojo over Slater Chatty Cathies, 90–82.
  • Biggest win: Arizona Cardinals over Philly Fly Eagles Fly, 108–49, a 59-point margin.

Scoreboard (with Totally Legit Vegas Lines™)

  • Big dog Fin 51 @ Arizona Wombats 106 → Arizona Wombats by 55
    line: Arizona Wombats -26.9
    The Wombats made it three straight weeks over 100. Finley’s 51 was his lowest total yet, and he is still looking for his first win. I would like to claim some sophisticated defensive strategy here, but mostly I submitted a lineup and watched his score stay small.
  • Arizona Smol Puppers 79 @ Big Dog D 118 → Big Dog D by 39
    line: Big Dog D -2
    Alana’s narrow escapes ended abruptly. After winning by five and three, she lost by 39 to a Davis team that jumped from 69 points to 118. Davis moves to 2–1 with the week’s high score; Alana joins him at 2–1 with a considerably less enjoyable explanation for how she got there.
  • Tempe Trout 98 @ Team Engelwoman 80 → Tempe Trout by 18
    line: Tempe Trout -15.3
    Andrea more than doubled last week’s 34. That is progress, though Glen would not let it become a win. Matthew Golden’s 16 sat on her bench behind a receiver who scored 6; that substitution alone would still have left her eight short. Glen improves to 2–1, and Andrea drops to 1–2 with a score she can at least bear to look at.
  • T-Dog’s Bruisers 101 @ Big Dog Will 84 → T-Dog’s Bruisers by 17
    line: Big Dog Will -5.4
    William followed his 92-point win with a 17-point loss. Tyson, meanwhile, followed 103 with 101 and collected a second consecutive victory. He left useful points on the bench from Jeremiyah Love and Sam Darnold, but the starters did enough. A pleasantly uneventful lineup review for a team called the Bruisers.
  • Slater Chatty Cathies 82 @ Pray for Mojo 90 → Pray for Mojo by 8
    line: Slater Chatty Cathies -4.1
    Phil handed Marliese her first loss in the closest game of the week. Her 133 from last week became 82, while Phil’s 90 was the lowest winning score this week. He has won two straight after losing the opener. Marliese still has two wins herself, but the opportunity to look down on the rest of us lasted exactly one newsletter.
  • Arizona Cardinals 108 @ Philly Fly Eagles Fly 49 → Arizona Cardinals by 59
    line: Arizona Cardinals -40.1
    Niklas scored 49 for the second week running. Ellis brought 108, which was more than sufficient and gave him the week’s largest margin. The Cardinals have climbed from 99 to 100 to 108; Philly’s immediate goal can be considerably more modest. Fifty would be a start.

Scoreboard lines are the predictions published in last week’s newsletter. Four favorites covered; Tyson and Phil won outright as underdogs.

Room at the top

  • Eight teams at 2–1: Arizona Wombats, Arizona Smol Puppers, Big Dog D, Tempe Trout, T-Dog’s Bruisers, Pray for Mojo, Slater Chatty Cathies, and Arizona Cardinals.
  • At 1–2: Big Dog Will and Team Engelwoman, both coming off a loss.
  • At 0–3: Big dog Fin and Philly Fly Eagles Fly. Finley draws Glen next, while Niklas gets Andrea. Neither needs a complicated scenario to improve his position; one win would do.
