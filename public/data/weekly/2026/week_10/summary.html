Four games left

The championship field remains three division winners plus one wildcard. Here’s what changed this week:

  • Shibas: Alana and the Wombats are 7–3, Davis is 6–4, and Finley is 4–6. All four won. Alana retains the division’s top position in ESPN’s ordering, with the Wombats currently in the wildcard spot. Davis is still one game back despite winning three straight. This division isn’t offering many easy ways to gain ground.
  • Unicorns: Glen is 6–4, Tyson and Andrea 4–6, and William 3–7. All four lost, so Glen keeps his two-game lead. Tyson’s 138 gets him no closer than Andrea’s 56, a fact I would suggest not bringing up at dinner.
  • Pandas: Phil and Marliese are 6–4, Ellis 5–5, and Niklas 2–8. ESPN now places Phil ahead of Marliese for the division’s playoff position. Ellis is only one game behind both of them after that 155-point return to form. Niklas is out of championship contention, but his remaining results still count for everybody he plays.

ESPN’s current top four seeds are Smol Puppers, Trout, Mojo, and Wombats, followed by Big Dog D and Chatty Cathies. These are current positions, not clinched places. Davis and Marliese are each one win behind the current wildcard record, while their division races offer another route in.

The league scored 1,175 points, up 88 from last week. Four teams reached 138 or better; Tyson was the only one of those four to lose. I think we’ve documented his grievance adequately now.
