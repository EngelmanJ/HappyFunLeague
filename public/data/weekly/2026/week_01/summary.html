Subject: 🏈 HFL Week 1 — 117 points ought to be enough

Owners of the Happy Fun League,

This recap is late. My complaint about the result is still timely.

The Wombats scored 117 points in Week 1. That would have beaten ten of the other eleven teams. We played Alana, who scored 122. Congratulations to the Smol Puppers, and please give me a little space while I explain how well my team played in a game it lost.

Highlights at a glance

  • Highest team score: Arizona Smol Puppers, 122.
  • Player of the Week: Caleb Williams, 36 points.
  • Closest game: Arizona Smol Puppers over Arizona Wombats, 122–117.
  • Biggest win: Tempe Trout over Big Dog Will, 111–77, a 34-point margin.

Scoreboard (with Totally Legit Vegas Lines™)

  • Arizona Wombats 117 @ Arizona Smol Puppers 122 → Arizona Smol Puppers by 5
    line: Smol Puppers -3.5
    The two highest scores in the league landed in the same matchup, and it was also the closest game of the week. Alana gets the win and the high score. I get enough supporting evidence to be annoying about this for several days.
  • Big Dog D 103 @ Big dog Fin 92 → Big Dog D by 11
    line: Big Dog D -3.5
    Davis opens with a win over Finley, despite leaving Christian Watson’s 26, Jalen Coker’s 25, and Kyle Monangai’s 18 on the bench. One starting receiver scored 2. Finley could reasonably have hoped that would matter more than it did.
    There will be plenty of time to question Davis’s lineup decisions. For now, he has a win and some interesting options for next week.
  • Big Dog Will 77 @ Tempe Trout 111 → Tempe Trout by 34
    line: Tempe Trout -3.5
    Glen wins by 34, the largest margin of the week. William left Tyler Shough’s 22 points behind a starting quarterback who scored 4. That’s an unpleasant 18-point difference, but even making that switch would have left him 16 short. Sometimes there isn’t one lineup decision you can blame, however convenient that would be.
  • Team Engelwoman 110 @ T-Dog’s Bruisers 78 → Team Engelwoman by 32
    line: Team Engelwoman -3.5
    Andrea finished one point behind Glen and won almost as comfortably, beating Tyson by 32. A straightforward, convincing start. I would like to point out that I scored seven more points than Andrea. I understand that nobody asked.
  • Pray for Mojo 81 @ Arizona Cardinals 99 → Arizona Cardinals by 18
    line: Cardinals -3.5
    Ellis had room to spare against Phil, even with Chuba Hubbard’s 19 on the bench and a starting running back at 6. The Cardinals stopped one point short of 100, which is aesthetically irritating but worth exactly the same win. Phil starts 0–1 with 81 points; there are worse opening weeks, and William and Tyson have supplied examples.
  • Slater Chatty Cathies 97 @ Philly Fly Eagles Fly 87 → Slater Chatty Cathies by 10
    line: Chatty Cathies -3.5
    Marliese beat Niklas by ten. Her 97 was the lowest winning score of the week, which is an efficient use of points and a subject I am currently unable to discuss generously. Niklas gets Pray for Mojo next; one of those teams will have a much nicer Week 2 recap.

One point worth mentioning

Caleb Williams scored 36, edging Josh Allen’s 35 for the week’s top player score. Derrick Henry was next at 33. Strong opening numbers, although I remain more interested in finding five additional Wombat points.

Our Totally Legit Vegas department issued the same -3.5 line for every game. Apparently the offseason research budget bought one number. All six listed favorites covered, so expect absolutely no improvement in the department’s attitude.
