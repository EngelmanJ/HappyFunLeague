Subject: 🏈 HFL Week 5 — Finley was saving up

Owners of the Happy Fun League,

Finley spent four weeks looking for a win, then scored 151. Apparently he had been waiting until he could make absolutely sure.

That was the highest team score of the season, and Andrea had the unfortunate distinction of being present for it. Davis also cleared the previous season high with 138. His timing was less considerate of his chances of getting the headline.
