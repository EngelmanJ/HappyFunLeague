Past halfway: the playoff picture

There are four championship places: three division winners and one wildcard. A good overall record matters, but so does which group you’re trying to win. Here’s where the three divisions stand:

  • Shibas: Alana and the Wombats are 6–3, Davis is 5–4, and Finley is 3–6. ESPN currently gives Alana the division’s top position and puts me in the wildcard slot. This is the only division with three teams holding winning records. Davis is one win back, so neither of us has much room to get comfortable.
  • Unicorns: Glen leads at 6–3, with Tyson and Andrea at 4–5 and William at 3–6. The Trout have the biggest division lead at two games. Tyson and Andrea both won this week, but Glen’s win kept them from gaining ground.
  • Pandas: Marliese is 6–3, Phil 5–4, Ellis 4–5, and Niklas 2–7. Marliese’s win and Phil’s loss broke their tie at the top. Phil remains only one game back; Ellis needs to interrupt that losing streak before the gap grows further.

The current ESPN seed order is Trout, Chatty Cathies, Smol Puppers, Wombats for the four playoff positions, followed by Mojo and Big Dog D. That’s today’s positioning, not a list of clinched berths. Five games remain, and three division races plus a single wildcard give us plenty to argue about before December.

Glen also moved three points ahead of the Wombats in season scoring, 928 to 925. Apparently sharing my record wasn’t sufficient.
